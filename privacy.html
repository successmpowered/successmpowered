Privacy

Privacy Policy

How we collect and use personal information, and the choices and rights you have.

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The information we collect

When you get in touch

If you contact us through our website, by email or on WhatsApp, we collect the details you share, such as your name, email address, phone number, organisation, role and message.

When you take one of our assessments

We record your answers, your score and your result. If you choose to unlock your full result, we also collect your name, email address, organisation and role, and whether you have agreed to hear from us.

When you book a call

Bookings are made through Calendly, which collects your name, email address, the time you choose and anything you add to the booking.

When you work with us

If you or your organisation engage us, or you take part in one of our programmes, we collect what we need to deliver that work: contact details, and information you share in conversations, feedback and surveys. Where your employer has engaged us, some of this may come from them.

When you visit our website

The services that deliver our website automatically process basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, so the site can load and stay secure. We don't use analytics or tracking tools. See Cookies below.

How we use it, and why

Data protection law requires a lawful basis for each use of personal information. Here is what we use yours for, and the basis we rely on.

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a conversation. Our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract.
  • To show you your assessment result. Our legitimate interest in providing the tool you chose to use.
  • To follow up with insights, resources or an invitation to talk. Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. You see your full assessment result whether or not you give it.
  • To deliver our services and manage our client relationships. Performance of a contract, or our legitimate interest in delivering work our clients have engaged us for.
  • To keep our website working and secure. Our legitimate interest in running a safe, reliable website.
  • To keep business records and meet our legal obligations, such as accounting and tax. Legal obligation.

We never sell personal information. Your assessment score is calculated automatically, but it is there for your own reflection and is never used to make decisions about you.

Who we share it with

Only with people who help us run our business or deliver our work, and only what they need:

  • the providers that host and deliver our website, including Cloudflare, which protects it from attacks
  • Calendly, for booking calls
  • our email provider, for receiving and replying to messages
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to message us there
  • senior associates who work with us on an engagement, under confidentiality
  • professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, and authorities where the law requires it

International transfers

Some of these providers store information outside the Isle of Man, the UK and the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. When information is transferred, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as adequacy arrangements or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and assessment details: two years from our last contact, unless you become a client.
  • Your consent to follow-up: until you withdraw it. After that, we keep only a record that you have opted out, so we don't contact you again.
  • Client and engagement records: six years after the engagement ends, to meet legal and accounting requirements.

After that, we delete the information or anonymise it.

Keeping it secure

We use reputable providers, limit access to the people who need it, and take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that is inaccurate
  • ask us to delete it
  • ask us to restrict or stop using it
  • object to our use of it based on legitimate interests, and object to direct marketing at any time
  • receive information you gave us in a portable format
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting anything we did before

To use any of these rights, email team@successmpowered.com. We will respond within one month. Every follow-up email we send also includes a way to unsubscribe.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please talk to us first and we will do our best to put it right.

You also have the right to complain to the Isle of Man Information Commissioner at inforights.im, by email at ask@inforights.im or by phone on +44 1624 693260. If you live in the UK or the EU, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

Cookies

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our website may use a small number of strictly necessary cookies, set by Cloudflare, to keep it secure. If you use our booking button, Calendly may set its own cookies; you can read about them in Calendly's privacy policy. You can also control cookies in your browser settings.

Children

Our website and services are for organisations and professionals, and are not intended for children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Who we are

Success Mpowered is a trading name of APOGEE LIMITED, a private company limited by shares, incorporated in the Isle of Man under the Companies Act 1931 (company registration number 132610C).

We handle personal information in line with Isle of Man data protection law, including the Data Protection Act 2018, the Data Protection (Application of GDPR) Order 2018 and the GDPR and LED Implementing Regulations 2018, and, where they apply to you, UK and EU data protection law.

For anything to do with your personal information, email team@successmpowered.com.
