Resilience Programme Global Technology Distributed Teams
01 of 03
IOHK
100% wanted the programme to continue

100% improved focus and concentration

Building personal resilience inside a high-pressure, globally distributed technology company.

IOHK is the global blockchain company behind the ADA cryptocurrency. Its teams worked remotely across time zones, under intense public pressure to deliver, with long hours and frequent organisational change. Leadership recognised what this was doing to people's physical, emotional and mental health. Slowing down was not an option, so they wanted to give their teams real support within the pace.

We began with the leadership team, agreeing what they wanted to achieve: practical ways to reduce stress, a shared positive experience for a remote global team, and a deeper understanding of where people's anxiety and frustration were coming from. We then held one-to-one conversations with team members to understand their individual challenges and expectations before designing anything.

The result was Boost Your Personal Resilience: one ninety-minute online session a week, timed to work across the team's time zones. Each session combined breathwork to reduce stress and sharpen focus, a short yoga sequence designed for office life, self-guided meditation, and awareness sessions that worked directly on the challenges people had raised.

"Honestly, I enjoyed the camaraderie and shared wellness exercises with my colleagues. I think it sends a really important message to each other that we value these types of exercises, modes of awareness and balance. And that we're not just saying we do."

Programme participant, IOHK

Motivation Workshop Managed Workspace Nordic Market
02 of 03
Regus Finland
100+ companies inside the Regus Finland network
Every day participants see the session's key takeaways, posted at reception

Helping the people inside a global co-working network face down the Nordic winter.

Regus Finland provides co-working and managed office space to more than 100 companies, and looks for ways to add value for the businesses in its buildings. Finnish winters are hard. The lack of sunlight is known to affect energy, motivation and mental health, and the whole working community feels it.

As the dark season approached, Regus asked us for a workshop that would help people sustain their motivation through it: something that lifted mood as well as productivity, and suited their brand and their customers.

We created Beat the Autumn Blues. It built on our signature motivation programme, which works from the inside out, and brought in guidance from nutritionists alongside research on the effects of low sunlight, reviewed by our experts. Participants left with practical tools and practices for keeping motivation up when the season works against it.

The session landed with the people who attended live and with those who watched the recording afterwards. Participants wrote down the three traps to avoid and three motivators from the session and posted them at reception, where they see them every day.

"At a time when the nights are dark and winters long the Power Skill Workshop on Motivation was extremely inspirational. We really enjoyed the program. It's been great working with Success Mpowered."

Regus Finland
Outcome

Participants described the session as interesting and useful, with practical tips they could use in everyday life. Its key points went up at reception, where participants see them every day: a small detail that says something about what the work left behind.

Leadership Development Psychological Safety Fintech, Washington DC
03 of 03
Lokyata
All participants would recommend the programme
C-suite piloted it first, before the wider team
Majority rated the programme excellent or good

Building psychological safety in a remote fintech team.

Lokyata is a Washington DC fintech whose data-driven tools help lenders automate their workflows and process more loans. After the pandemic, with everyone working remotely, keeping people motivated and connected had become harder. Work had narrowed to individual tasks on individual screens, in a business that depends on constant innovation and collaboration.

Lokyata wanted a programme that would protect its people-first ethos. Management was sceptical at first, so we piloted the programme with the CEO, COO, CTO and Head of Operations. Only once they were convinced did we take it to the rest of the company.

One-to-one conversations with team members shaped what came next. People wanted better ways to make decisions and look after their mental health, tools for handling the frustrations of day-to-day work, stronger connection with colleagues, and clearer communication. The programme worked on self-motivation, letting emotions pass before making decisions, an inner sense of purpose, and locus of control, supported by exercises and worksheets between sessions and examples drawn from their own working world.

Participants described becoming less conflict-driven in disagreements, more careful to give others space to speak, and more responsive rather than reactive. Some of the more junior team members said little in the sessions at first. In the one-to-ones afterwards, they turned out to be the ones completing the most exercises and putting the learning into practice week after week.

"It was extremely helpful for me as it helped me look at myself, become more aware of myself and my own emotions and understand what others might be going through. These topics helped my staff to be able to talk freely, avoid fear and talk about things with no fear of others judging them."

Santosh, CEO, Lokyata
